01  Overview

An identity that reaches past the logo - into cloth, paper and the way things are wrapped.

Design Tales by Hinal Bangera pairs a classical serif with a flowing script, adds a DT monogram and a botanical line drawing, and carries them in burgundy, taupe and ivory across garments, packaging and print.

02  Identity

Primary Logo

The wordmark sets the name in serif capitals, signed below in script. The monogram - a serif D crossed by a single script stroke - carries the brand where the full name won't fit.

03Colour

Burgundy #5B1F24, vintage taupe #A79A8D and ivory #F5EFE7 - shown with the linen, paper and stone they sit beside.

05  Botanical language

A single leaf, drawn in fine line and left to run.

The linework repeats as an all-over surface - on tissue, envelope liners, box lids and the backs of swing tags.

Wrapping

Ivory tissue in burgundy line, closed with a burgundy card.
